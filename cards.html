Proposed operating model for the Harvard International Office

Work sample by Narissara Khomkhai · Application for Associate Director of Operations & Administration

Synthetic data · not an HIO or Harvard document
HIO baseline: 10,158 visa-sponsored students and scholars (5,627 students, 4,531 scholars) from 150 countries, from HIO's statistics page (hio.harvard.edu/statistics), which notes the counts are for reference and does not stamp a year on them. All other HIO figures are synthetic.
Published context: Open Doors 2026 reports 1,146,163 international students in the U.S. in 2025/26, down 2.7%, with new international students down 17.7% and OPT up 14.1% to 335,785. IIE's fall 2026 snapshot shows total international students down 9% and new students down 15%. Harvard reported a record 6,749 international students in fall 2025, 28% of enrollment. OPT is tracked as its own flow for that reason, and card 11 turns these figures into a three-year outlook.
