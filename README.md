# HIO Operations and Administration · work sample

Work sample by Narissara Khomkhai for the Harvard International Office **Associate Director of Operations & Administration** application (Requisition 004436SR).

- `index.html` interactive work sample organized by eight work areas, with SMARTER inside each area popup and seven sample reports
- Navigation organized around the eight work areas; SMARTER is the checklist inside each area, not a top-level section
- Geography atlas: published OIRA country-by-School data for 2016–2025 plus the limited historical HIO China/India series for 2004–2010; filters for series, year and School, an actual country map and country ranking
- `atlas-data.js` and `atlas.js` contain the atlas data, map geometry and interaction logic
- `career-profile.html` HIO career profile built on the original full career-profile layout: portrait, interactive timeline, role-mix chart, conversion flows, DataHub/Advancement graphics, career artifacts, HIO fit, credentials and the 120-hour automation roadmap
- Section-by-section paging: scroll-snap stops at each section, with a fixed previous/next pager and section count
- `application/` contains the tailored HIO resume and cover letter in DOCX and PDF, plus the reusable generator script
- `CLONE.md` explains how to reuse the work sample for another organization, role or project
- First 90 days includes a detailed 13-week calendar, an eight-area gate register and daily/weekly/monthly/gate reporting cadence
- Common issues redesigned as an issue register: summary counts, category index, status, problem, repeatable response, decision owner and closure evidence
- Scope section includes a proposed decision-rights table: what the Associate Director owns, what is recommended to the Director, and what stays with HUIT, Finance, OGC or ITCRB
- Program and project management section: 12-month three-tranche portfolio, benefits register, program board, capacity/dependency rules, then the 90-day project charter, WBS, critical path, RAID log, change control, stakeholder cadence and closure
- Newcomer services queue: nine sample records with intake owner, assigned advisor, stage, progress and next action; filters for unassigned, assigned, needs correction and past due
- Each work-area popup now has a command center with simulated progress tracking, an automation flow with a human checkpoint, RACI and a delivery gate, plus a section-level portfolio progress summary
- Sample reports now print one per page: `?print=reports` builds seven scaled Letter pages with the synthetic-data label in each header; `HIO_Sample_Reports.pdf` is the regenerated seven-page version
- Automation roadmap infographic in Delivery: five builds over 12 weeks with cumulative hours saved to 120 a month
- Modernization popup aligned to HUIT Enterprise Architecture/HEART, ITCRB/MYTP, security, accessibility, AI governance and vendor review
- AI triage concept with RAG citations, public-content-only scope, human handoff and evaluation; no live case advice
- Work-sample-only content: no years of experience, citizenship, prior-employer metrics or resume-style evidence; those belong in a separate career profile
- Scope & assumptions section explains role assumptions, access, team size and evidence standard, and keeps the working organization chart
- Country data section: OIRA 2019-20 to 2025-26 unique nonresident totals, three planning scenarios, and an explanation of the OIRA country × School denominator problem
- Workflow management board (one queue, six stages, WIP limits) and a stress-test plan with sample peak scenarios
- Registration report includes a student ID / advisor follow-up queue, presented as a filtered view of ISSM/ISD rather than a second tracker
- `HIO_Work_Sample.pdf` legacy print version; not linked from the page because it may contain career-profile material. Regenerate before sharing.
- `HIO_Sample_Reports.pdf` the seven reports

All figures except HIO's published counts are synthetic. This is not a Harvard document.

The web version is the current one. Federal-policy statuses were verified on **November 24, 2026**; recheck before relying on them.
